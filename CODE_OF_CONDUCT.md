# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members of the community pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for yourself
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, and the impact of our actions on others

## Our Responsibilities

Community members have the responsibility to report behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in project-related venues.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, or other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
